Ready to Upgrade Your Home Tech?

Contact us today for residential networking support, smart home assistance, computer troubleshooting, and consumer IT solutions.

Service area: Harrisburg, York, Carlisle, Lancaster & surrounding central PA.

Home Networking — What we do

We audit your current setup, optimize Wi‑Fi coverage using channel planning and placement, recommend and install mesh or business-grade systems, run Ethernet where needed, and secure your network with guest segmentation and password policies.

Typical visit: Network survey, router/mesh setup, speed testing, and brief user training.

Computer Support — What we do

We diagnose software and hardware issues, remove malware, perform performance tuning, recommend and install upgrades, back up important data, and configure systems for better reliability and speed.

Typical visit: Full diagnostic, cleanup, and actionable recommendations.

Smart Home Setup — What we do

We connect and configure smart devices (TVs, cameras, speakers, thermostats), integrate automations, ensure secure device onboarding, and help you choose compatible ecosystems for long-term reliability.

Typical visit: Device integration, testing, and user walkthrough of automations.

Device Repair — What we do

We perform hardware diagnostics, replace or repair components where feasible, reimage devices, and provide guidance on economical replacement versus repair options.

Typical visit: Diagnostic and repair estimate; minor repairs performed on-site when possible.
